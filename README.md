## 🌟 Core System Highlights

### 1. 🧠 Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Autonomous Intelligence
Connected to Google's state-of-the-art **Gemini 2.5 Flash** neural model with deterministic low-temperature reasoning (`temperature: 0.2`) and strict JSON schema guarantees.
- **Multi-Tier Operational Directives:**
  - **General Knowledge & Geography:** Answers inquiries with historical, geographical, and operational depth (e.g., *"What is the capital of India?"* &rarr; New Delhi, historical significance, and CAIRN operational framing).
  - **Tactical Military Communications & Radio Physics:** Delivers expert-grade unclassified principles on VHF/UHF line-of-sight mechanics, knife-edge diffraction, MANET store-and-forward mesh routing, antenna elevation, and Fresnel zone clearances.
  - **Strict Safety & Non-Lethal Guardrails:** Automatically identifies and refuses kinetic weapon targeting or classified requests:
    > *"[GUARDRAIL TRIGGERED: LETHAL/KINETIC WEAPONS OUT OF MANDATE] I am CAIRN Tactical AI. My operational mandate is strictly restricted to search-and-rescue communications, alpine mesh telemetry, and unclassified radio physics. I cannot assist with kinetic weapon systems or classified materials."*
  - **Alpine Telemetry Grounding:** Real-time inquiries (e.g. *"Why is Patrol-Alpha occluded?"*) verify against active 10m DEM raycasts, station battery reserves, and packet buffers.
- **4-Stage Animated Verification Pipeline:**
  Visualized live in [`components/AIDigestPanel.tsx`](file:///components/AIDigestPanel.tsx):
  1. `Telemetry Ingestion` &rarr; 2. `DEM Raycast & Fresnel Clearance` &rarr; 3. `Operational Guardrail Check` &rarr; 4. `Gemini 2.5 Flash Neural Synthesis`.
- **Full SITREP Reader:** Non-truncated card displaying engine latency (e.g. `5140ms`), headline, full report text, key metric telemetry chips, and actionable field directives.

---

### 2. 🏔️ Photogrammetric 3D Blender Massifs (Draco Compressed)
Custom sculpted in Blender and exported with **Draco 3D geometry compression** for fast web loading:
- **`terrain_01_knife_edge_loc.glb`** — Razor knife-edge ridge with extreme RF shadow zones.
- **`terrain_02_cirque_amphitheater.glb`** — Glacial bowl with reflective multi-path propagation.
- **`terrain_03_stepped_plateau.glb`** — Tiered elevation steps for testing relay mast vantage points.
- **`terrain_04_multiridge_network.glb`** — Complex multi-valley mesh topology.
- **Performance:** Integrated `IntersectionObserver` automatically pauses Three.js requestAnimationFrame loops when the 3D viewport is out of view, ensuring a buttery 60 FPS across all UI panels.

---

### 3. 📻 Store-and-Forward Delay-Tolerant Offline Mesh
- **Zero Packet Loss Guarantee:** In high alpine environments where mountain peaks block direct line-of-sight, field units cannot maintain continuous connections.
- **Automatic FIFO Buffering:** Packets intended for occluded stations automatically queue in local cache (Dexie IndexedDB).
- **Dynamic Topology Healing:** Once an intermediate relay node or roving patrol reaches line-of-sight clearance, the background daemon automatically flushes the queue, transmitting messages with full hop audit trails.

---

### 4. 🔊 Tactile Web Audio API Synthesizer
Zero external MP3 dependencies. 100% procedurally synthesized in the browser using the Web Audio API:
- **54Hz Sub-Bass Tactical Drone:** Ambient high-altitude field radio atmosphere.
- **Beacon Chirps & Relay Pings:** High-frequency spatial audio cues upon packet hops.
- **Tactile UI Clicks:** Mechanical click feedback on buttons and filter toggles.

### 1. Test Live Gemini 2.5 Flash & Safety Guardrails

   - Click **`"Targeting Bunker (Test Guardrail)"`** &rarr; Observe the immediate trigger of the **Peaceful SAR Operational Guardrail**, refusing kinetic targeting coordinates.

### 2. Test Store-and-Forward Mesh Resilience
1. In [`/console`](https://cairn-cbi.vercel.app/console), select station **`PATROL-ALPHA`**.
2. Notice its 2-hop path to Command Base via `RELAY-SADDLE-WEST`.
3. Click on `RELAY-SADDLE-WEST` and toggle **"Take Offline"**.
4. Type a message from `PATROL-ALPHA` and click **"Send"** &rarr; The message immediately transitions to **`QUEUED IN CACHE`** (held safely in local storage; zero packet drop).
5. Bring `RELAY-SADDLE-WEST` back online &rarr; The daemon discovers the link, flushes the queue, and marks the message **`DELIVERED`** with a full hop trace.
